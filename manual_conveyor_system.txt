Equipment Manual: Belt Conveyor System Model BC-500 (Doc ID: EM-077)

1. Overview
The BC-500 belt conveyor system transports finished components between the assembly
line and the packaging station at a rated capacity of 45 units per minute.

2. Startup Procedure
2.1 Ensure all emergency stop buttons along the conveyor are in the released position.
2.2 Check that the belt tension indicator shows a reading within the green zone.
2.3 Power on the main drive motor from the control panel and observe the belt for
smooth, even tracking for at least 60 seconds before loading material.

3. Routine Maintenance
3.1 Lubricate the drive chain every 500 operating hours using the specified chain
lubricant (see Appendix A for approved lubricants).
3.2 Inspect belt tracking weekly. Misalignment greater than 10 mm to either side
requires adjustment of the tracking rollers.
3.3 Check motor temperature monthly using an infrared thermometer. Normal operating
temperature is 40-60 degrees Celsius. Temperatures above 75 degrees Celsius indicate
a possible bearing failure or overload condition.

4. Common Faults and Resolutions
4.1 Belt slipping: usually caused by insufficient tension or a worn drive pulley.
Increase tension incrementally and inspect pulley surface for glazing.
4.2 Excessive noise: often caused by a misaligned idler roller or a failing bearing.
Isolate the noise source by listening at each roller station while running at low speed.
4.3 Belt tracking to one side: adjust the tail pulley alignment in small increments and
re-check tracking after each adjustment.

5. Shutdown Procedure
5.1 Clear all material from the belt.
5.2 Power down the drive motor from the control panel.
5.3 Engage the mechanical brake if the conveyor is on an incline section.

6. Safety Notes
Do not reach into the conveyor while it is running. All guards must be reinstalled
before restarting after maintenance.
