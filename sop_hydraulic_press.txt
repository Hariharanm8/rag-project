Standard Operating Procedure: Hydraulic Press Maintenance (Doc ID: SOP-204)

1. Purpose
This procedure describes the routine maintenance steps for the 200-ton hydraulic press
used in the stamping line. Failure to follow this procedure may result in equipment
downtime or safety hazards.

2. Safety Precautions
Before performing any maintenance, the press must be locked out and tagged out (LOTO)
in accordance with plant safety policy. All hydraulic pressure must be released from the
system before opening any hydraulic lines. Operators must wear safety glasses and
insulated gloves when handling hydraulic fluid.

3. Weekly Maintenance Steps
3.1 Check hydraulic fluid level using the sight glass on the reservoir. Fluid should be
between the MIN and MAX marks.
3.2 Inspect all hydraulic hoses for cracking, bulging, or leaks. Replace any hose showing
signs of wear immediately.
3.3 Check the pressure relief valve setting using the calibrated gauge. The relief valve
should trigger at 3000 PSI +/- 50 PSI.
3.4 Clean the return line filter. Replace the filter element if pressure drop exceeds 15 PSI.

4. Monthly Maintenance Steps
4.1 Drain and inspect hydraulic fluid for contamination or discoloration.
4.2 Test the emergency stop function and verify response time is under 0.5 seconds.
4.3 Inspect ram alignment and check for excessive play in the guide rails.

5. Troubleshooting
If the press fails to build pressure, check for air in the system, a failing pump, or a
stuck relief valve. If cycle time has increased significantly, inspect the pump for wear
and check for internal leakage past the piston seals.

6. Record Keeping
All maintenance activities must be logged in the CMMS system with technician name,
date, and parts replaced.
