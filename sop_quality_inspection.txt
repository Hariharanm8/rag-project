Quality Inspection Procedure: Incoming Raw Material Inspection (Doc ID: QP-118)

1. Scope
This procedure applies to all incoming raw steel coils received from approved suppliers
before they are released to production.

2. Inspection Equipment Required
Calipers, micrometer, hardness tester, coating thickness gauge, and the material
certificate provided by the supplier.

3. Inspection Steps
3.1 Verify the material certificate matches the purchase order specification, including
grade, thickness, and width tolerance.
3.2 Measure thickness at five points across the coil width. Acceptable tolerance is
+/- 0.05 mm from nominal.
3.3 Perform a hardness test at three locations. Hardness must fall within the range
specified for the material grade (typically 120-150 HB for the standard grade used).
3.4 Visually inspect for surface defects such as scratches, rust, or edge cracks. Reject
any coil with defects exceeding 5% of surface area.
3.5 Check coating thickness where applicable using the coating gauge. Minimum
acceptable coating is 20 microns.

4. Disposition
4.1 If all checks pass, tag the coil as "Released" and update the inventory system.
4.2 If any check fails, tag the coil as "On Hold" and notify the quality manager for
further review. A Non-Conformance Report (NCR) must be raised within 24 hours.

5. Common Non-Conformances
The most frequent non-conformances observed over the past year are thickness
deviation (38% of cases) and surface defects (29% of cases), followed by hardness
out-of-range (18%) and coating deficiency (15%).

6. Documentation
All inspection results must be recorded in the Quality Management System (QMS) with
photographs attached for any rejected material.
